# Chat

A session's conversation end to end, laid out like the chats people already use: your messages are bubbles on the right (their time on hover), the agent's replies on the left with its mark in a narrow gutter. Earlier turns are a quiet “Worked …” line plus their answer, always in full, and the files they changed, so you can scan what was asked and what came back; hovering one shows its facts and actions. The live turn shows what the agent says and does as it goes, its current action and its reasoning; when it finishes it folds in place, the answer completes and the changed files appear. `TurnNav` sits at the edge. The chat scrolls in `ChatScroll`: while a reply has pushed your message out of view, that message stays pinned at the top, with Back to it and Hide.

Press **Replay** to watch a turn think, talk, act and fold.
